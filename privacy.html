Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This application is a private, single-user integration used to connect rclone with the application owner's Google Drive account.

The application does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information to an external service operated by the application owner.

Google account authorization tokens and rclone configuration data are stored locally on the application owner's computer. Access to Google Drive is used only to perform file-management operations explicitly initiated or configured by the owner through rclone.

Access can be revoked at any time through the Google Account security settings.
